01. Meet Maya: Your First Python Program¶

Suggested time: 45–60 minutes. Work through lessons in order; each notebook includes its own runnable examples.

All people, policies, prices, and records are fictional. Amounts use Philippine pesos (₱ / PHP). This is a coding lesson, not financial advice. Real coverage and claims depend on policy terms and applicable law. Never enter personal financial or health information.

Story opening¶

Maya hears her aunt talking about life insurance at dinner. She wants to explain it to Leo without using difficult words. Her first program will introduce the family and explain who is covered.

Learning goals¶

Print messages; write comments; recognize strings.

Insurance in everyday words¶

Life insurance is an agreement with an insurer (the insurance company). Someone pays premiums, and the insurer pays a benefit if the insured person dies while covered, subject to the policy terms. The insured person is the person whose life is covered. The person paying and the insured person need not be the same.

Python toolbox¶

Code cells contain instructions. Click a cell and press Shift+Enter to run it. print() displays a message. Text inside quotes is a string. A line beginning with # is a comment for readers; Python ignores it.
